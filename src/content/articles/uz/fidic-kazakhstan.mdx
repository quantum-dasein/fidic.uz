FIDIC Kazakhstan — alohida qidiruv intenti: pudratchi, konsultant va buyurtmachilar nazariya emas, xalqaro forma Qozog‘iston loyihasida qanday ishlashini izlaydi.

## Tenderdan oldin

Kim loyihalashtiradi, ground risk qanday taqsimlangan, qaysi guarantees talab qilinadi, delay damages qanday cap bilan cheklangan va claims procedure ishlay oladimi — shularni tekshiring. EPC loyihalarda Silver Book va fitness for purpose wording juda muhim.

## Awarddan keyin

Awarddan keyin Contract Data, Particular Conditions, notices, Sub-Clause 20.2, DAAB/arbitration, payment currency va majburiy mahalliy talablarni ko‘rib chiqing. MDB loyihasida bank-specific procurement va integrity shartlarini alohida o‘qing.
