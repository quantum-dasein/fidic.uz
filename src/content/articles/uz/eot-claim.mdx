EOT claim — “muddatni uzaytirib bering” degan xat emas. Bu dalillar tizimi: hodisa, shartnomaviy huquq, o‘z vaqtida notice, critical path ta’siri va hisoblangan davr.

## Huquqiy mantiq

Sub-Clause 8.5 Extension of Time huquqini beradi, Sub-Clause 20.2 esa procedure belgilaydi. Birinchi xavf — 28 kunlik notice. U o‘tkazib yuborilsa, faktlar kuchli bo‘lsa ham EOT huquqi yo‘qolishi mumkin.

## Texnik mantiq

EOT programme orqali isbotlanadi: baseline, updates, faktik sanalar, windows/time-slice yoki boshqa delay-analysis method. Employer delay contractor concurrent delaydan ajratilishi kerak.
