“Red Book vs Yellow Book vs Silver Book” akademik savol emas. Bu risk allocation qarori. Noto‘g‘ri kitob birinchi claimdan oldin ham loyihani qimmatlashtiradi.

## Tez mantiq

- **Red Book**: buyurtmachi design qiladi, to‘lov re-measurement bo‘yicha.
- **Yellow Book**: pudratchi design-build qiladi, odatda lump sum.
- **Silver Book**: EPC/turnkey, buyurtmachi uchun narx va muddat aniqligi yuqori.

## Nima solishtiriladi

Faqat kitob nomiga qaramang. Design responsibility, site data, approvals, unforeseeable conditions, testing, performance guarantees, LD cap va dispute pathni tekshiring.
