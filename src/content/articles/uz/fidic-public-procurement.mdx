Davlat xaridlarida FIDIC submissiondan oldingi intizomni talab qiladi. Awarddan keyin ko‘p risklar allaqachon pudratchi narxiga aylangan bo‘ladi.

## Nima tekshiriladi

Instructions to Bidders, Conditions of Contract, Contract Data, Employer’s Requirements, specifications, drawings, BoQ, guarantee forms va evaluation criteriani solishtiring. Har qanday konflikt og‘zaki taxmin emas, clarification question bo‘lishi kerak.

## Tipik risk zonalari

- hujjatlar ustuvorligi noaniq;
- BoQ va drawings mos emas;
- ortiqcha performance/security guarantees;
- Particular Conditionsda yashirin risk transfer;
- MDB procurement talablari va mahalliy huquq birga ishlashi.
