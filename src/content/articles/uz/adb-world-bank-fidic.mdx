ADB va World Bank FIDIC loyihalari standart forma va bank qoidalari kesishgan joyda turadi. Bitta ilovadagi xato claims, payment yoki compliance riskiga aylanishi mumkin.

## Asosiy hujjatlar

Conditions of Contract, COPA / Particular Conditions, Contract Data, Employer’s Requirements, guarantee forms, procurement rules, ESHS requirements va fraud/corruption clausesni tekshiring.

## Tipik savollar

- General Conditions o‘zgartiriladimi;
- site data va approvals riskini kim oladi;
- payment cycle va deductions qanday ishlaydi;
- DAAB va arbitration qanday tuzilgan;
- claims va suspension uchun qaysi notices majburiy.
