“Red Book vs Yellow Book vs Silver Book” is not an academic question. It is a risk-allocation decision. The wrong book can make the project expensive before the first claim.

## Fast logic

- **Red Book**: employer designs, payment by re-measurement.
- **Yellow Book**: contractor designs and builds, usually lump sum.
- **Silver Book**: EPC/turnkey, maximum price and time certainty for the employer.

## What to compare

Do not compare the book names only. Check design responsibility, site data, approvals, unforeseeable conditions, testing, performance guarantees, LD cap and dispute path.
