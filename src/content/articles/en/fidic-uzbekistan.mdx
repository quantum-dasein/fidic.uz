FIDIC in Uzbekistan works best not as a translated template, but as a managed contract system: the FIDIC book, Particular Conditions, Contract Data, local law, procurement rules and project records have to fit together.

## What to check first

- the book matches the delivery model: Red, Yellow, Silver, Pink or Green;
- Particular Conditions do not silently transfer risk or conflict with law;
- Contract Data covers notices, guarantees, LD cap, time limits and DAAB;
- the project team understands [Sub-Clause 20.2](/en/clauses/claims-20-2/) and the 28/84-day cycle;
- there is a live register for notices, events, programmes and contemporary records.

## Uzbek context

The local market is not only about English General Conditions. You need to check permits, currency and payment, tax impact, bank guarantees, public procurement rules, MDB conditions and dispute resolution. See the deeper page: [FIDIC and Uzbek law](/en/knowledge/fidic-and-uzbek-law/).

Start with the [Book Selector](/en/tools/book-selector/), test the tender package in [Tender Risk Lab](/en/tools/tender-risk-lab/) and set up notices through [Notice Deadline](/en/tools/notice-deadline/). If the project is already contentious, open [Claim Readiness](/en/tools/claim-readiness/).
