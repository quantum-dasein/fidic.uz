FIDIC Kazakhstan is a distinct search intent: contractors, consultants and employers are not looking for generic FIDIC theory, but for how the international form behaves in a Kazakh project.

## Before tender

Check who designs, how ground risk is allocated, which guarantees are required, how delay damages are capped and whether the claims procedure can actually work. For EPC projects, Silver Book and fitness-for-purpose wording are critical.

## After award

After award, review Contract Data, Particular Conditions, notices, Sub-Clause 20.2, DAAB/arbitration, payment currency and mandatory local requirements. If the project is MDB-financed, read the bank-specific procurement and integrity conditions.
